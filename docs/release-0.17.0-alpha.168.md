# 0.17.0-alpha.168 — continuous vehicle SoC estimation

A Mii observation under alpha.167 showed genuine increasing charger energy and roughly 1.53 kW charging while the EMS SoC was held at 34.47%. The retained cloud report was 27%; the estimator stopped extending its anchor after two hours with retained-estimate:basis-invalid-or-stale. This is the diagnosed software time limit, not proof of a charger fault or the vehicle's true current SoC.

The estimator now keeps a separate persisted continuity watermark for the original aligned anchor. Valid counter observations, monotonic energy, at most 60-second source/observation gaps and the existing connection checks allow interpolation beyond two hours. The original anchor timestamp and energy are not advanced, preventing double counting. The 256-sample bound remains. Normal short restarts preserve continuity; longer unobserved intervals do not prove it.

Missing/bad-quality/unacknowledged/stale energy, inconsistent frames, unknown report/settings or invalid explicit measurement time invalidate extended continuity. Counter reset, implausible jumps, observed disconnect/reconnect and changed source/settings retain their existing protections. Authoritative new vehicle reports still correct the estimate; matching rounded reports may preserve fractional progress when continuity is proven. Raw report age stays explicit and the value remains an estimate, capped at 100%. SoCStatus now explicitly distinguishes ongoing energy interpolation from a held old estimate, including with an aged cloud report.

Legacy ledgers contain no such continuity proof. An expired legacy anchor is not retroactively authorized from current energy alone. The already frozen installation may need one new time-aligned vehicle report after updating. No cloud refresh command, synthetic SoC, counter reset or plant write is issued by this change. Within the previous two-hour window, legacy fallback behavior remains compatible; this fix adds guarded long-session continuation rather than erasing existing source checks.

Verification includes a five-hour Mii-like 1.53-kW session, bounded ring and restart/idempotency, gaps/quality/echo/stale/inconsistent samples, legacy or mismatched watermarks, new lower/rounded reports and reset/disconnect. Full suite, lint, package/schema, installed tarball and historical comparisons are required. No complete real charging acceptance, price-policy change or new SQL/history query is implied.
